What happens to the spacecraft and equipment we leave behind on the Moon and Mars?

Many spacecraft and equipment are left some where in the outer space abandoned and forgotten.

Though their active missions ended long ago, these machines are abandoned but not forgotten; they remain standing as solitary monuments to human curiosity, enduring proof of the leaps we dared to take, and silent storytellers of the science that got us there.
